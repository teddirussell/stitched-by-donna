Hello, I'm Donna

I sew from home in Alstonville, piecing, paper piecing and quilting everything in 100% cotton. Christmas is my favourite season to sew for: cheeky reindeer, koalas in Santa hats, and treat bags for every little person on your list.

Most of my work finds a home through word of mouth and Facebook. This page puts it all in one place.
